# 📈 Correlation Analysis

## Overview

Correlation analysis was conducted to investigate the relationship between sanitation access and public health outcomes across districts of Tamil Nadu. The objective was to determine whether improvements in sanitation infrastructure are associated with better health indicators and reduced disease prevalence.

This analysis forms the statistical foundation of the research and helps identify the strength and direction of relationships between sanitation, health, and socio-demographic variables.

---

# 🎯 Purpose of Correlation Analysis

The primary objectives of the correlation analysis were to:

* Measure relationships between sanitation indicators and public health outcomes.
* Identify factors influencing disease prevalence.
* Understand how socio-demographic variables affect sanitation effectiveness.
* Support evidence-based policy recommendations.
* Validate patterns observed through GIS mapping.

---

# 📊 Statistical Technique Used

The study employed the **Pearson Correlation Coefficient (r)** to measure the strength and direction of relationships between variables.

## Interpretation of Correlation Values

| Correlation Value | Interpretation                |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------- |
| +1.0              | Perfect Positive Correlation  |
| +0.7 to +0.99     | Strong Positive Correlation   |
| +0.3 to +0.69     | Moderate Positive Correlation |
| 0                 | No Correlation                |
| -0.3 to -0.69     | Moderate Negative Correlation |
| -0.7 to -0.99     | Strong Negative Correlation   |
| -1.0              | Perfect Negative Correlation  |

### Meaning

* Positive values indicate that variables increase together.
* Negative values indicate that one variable decreases as the other increases.
* Values closer to ±1 indicate stronger relationships.

# 🔗 Relationship Between Sanitation Coverage and Infant Mortality

One of the strongest relationships observed during the analysis was between sanitation coverage and infant mortality.

## Finding

A strong negative correlation was identified between:

* Toilet Coverage
* Infant Mortality Rate (IMR)

### Interpretation

As sanitation coverage increases:

* Infant mortality decreases.
* Exposure to environmental pathogens is reduced.
* Child health outcomes improve.

This suggests that sanitation infrastructure plays an important role in reducing preventable infant deaths.

---

# 🦠 Relationship Between Sanitation and Waterborne Diseases

The analysis also examined sanitation accessibility and disease prevalence.

## Finding

Districts with improved sanitation access generally exhibit:

* Lower diarrheal disease incidence
* Reduced cholera prevalence
* Better environmental hygiene conditions

### Interpretation

Improved sanitation reduces contamination pathways and minimizes the spread of waterborne diseases.

---

# 🚫 Open Defecation and Public Health Risks

Open defecation remains one of the most critical sanitation challenges.

## Finding

Positive correlations were observed between:

* Open Defecation Rate
* Disease Incidence

Higher open defecation levels correspond to:

* Increased disease burden
* Poor environmental conditions
* Higher public health risks

### Interpretation

These findings emphasize the importance of behavioral change initiatives alongside sanitation infrastructure development.

---

# 📚 Literacy and Sanitation Adoption

Education was found to significantly influence sanitation effectiveness.

## Finding

A positive relationship exists between:

* Literacy Rate
* Sanitation Usage

Districts with higher literacy levels generally demonstrate:

* Better sanitation practices
* Greater awareness of hygiene
* Lower disease prevalence

### Interpretation

Educational attainment contributes to improved sanitation adoption and health outcomes.

---

# 💰 Income and Health Outcomes

Income was evaluated as a socio-economic factor influencing sanitation accessibility.

## Finding

Districts with higher per capita income generally show:

* Greater sanitation access
* Better healthcare accessibility
* Improved health indicators

### Interpretation

Economic development supports both sanitation infrastructure and healthcare utilization.

---

# 🏙️ Urbanization and Sanitation Access

Urbanization also influences sanitation outcomes.

## Finding

Urban districts typically exhibit:

* Higher sanitation coverage
* Better waste management systems
* Improved health outcomes

However, rapid urban growth can create localized sanitation challenges in densely populated regions.

### Interpretation

Urbanization can improve sanitation accessibility when supported by effective infrastructure planning.

# ⚠️ Correlation Does Not Imply Causation

Although strong relationships were identified, it is important to note that:

### Correlation Measures Association

Correlation indicates that variables move together.

### Correlation Does Not Prove Cause

Additional factors may influence observed relationships, including:

* Healthcare accessibility
* Environmental conditions
* Population density
* Government interventions
* Economic development

Future research should incorporate longitudinal and causal modeling approaches.
