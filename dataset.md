# 📖 Overview

This dataset was developed to support the research project:

> **"Correlation Mapping of Sanitation Access and Public Health Outcomes in Tamil Nadu"**

The dataset integrates sanitation, health, socio-demographic, and spatial indicators collected from multiple government and public data repositories.

The primary objective is to investigate how sanitation infrastructure and accessibility influence public health outcomes across districts of Tamil Nadu through statistical analysis and Geographic Information System (GIS) mapping.

The compiled dataset enables researchers, policymakers, and students to perform:

* 📊 Statistical Analysis
* 📈 Regression Modeling
* 🗺️ GIS Mapping
* 📍 Spatial Correlation Analysis
* 🏥 Public Health Assessment
* 📑 Policy Evaluation

---

# 🌍 Study Area

## Location

**Tamil Nadu, India**

Tamil Nadu is one of India's most urbanized and industrialized states, making it an ideal case study for understanding the relationship between sanitation infrastructure and public health outcomes.

### Geographic Scope

* State: Tamil Nadu
* Country: India
* Administrative Level: District
* Coverage: All Tamil Nadu Districts

### Unit of Analysis

The primary unit of analysis is the **district level**, allowing comparison between different regions and identification of localized sanitation and health disparities.

# 🚻 Sanitation Indicators

Sanitation indicators measure the availability and accessibility of sanitation facilities across districts.

## Variables Included

* Household Toilet Coverage (%)
* Open Defecation Rate
* Community Toilet Availability
* Sanitation Infrastructure Coverage
* Open Defecation Free (ODF) Status
* Access to Improved Sanitation Facilities

## Importance

These indicators help evaluate:

* Sanitation accessibility
* Infrastructure effectiveness
* Regional sanitation disparities
* Behavioral sanitation patterns

## Data Sources

### Swachh Bharat Mission (SBM)

Provides:

* Toilet construction statistics
* ODF status reports
* District-level sanitation coverage

### National Family Health Survey (NFHS-5)

Provides:

* Household sanitation access
* Hygiene indicators
* Family-level sanitation characteristics

### Tamil Nadu Open Government Data Portal

Provides:

* District sanitation statistics
* Public infrastructure records

# ⚠️ Dataset Limitations

Although extensive efforts were made to ensure data quality, several limitations should be considered.

### Administrative Aggregation

District-level analysis may hide local variations at village or block levels.

### Self-Reported Information

Certain variables originate from survey responses and may contain reporting bias.

### Temporal Variability

Data sources may correspond to different reporting years.

### Spatial Accuracy

GIS datasets may contain minor boundary inconsistencies.

### Correlation Limitations

Observed relationships indicate association and should not be interpreted as direct causation.
